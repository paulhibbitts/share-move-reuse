Built in, for different kinds of readers:

- **For keyboard users:** a "Skip to content" link, arrow-key Previous/Next, **/** for search, and wide tables you can reach with Tab
- **For screen reader users:** one title per page, labelled regions and links, titled embeds, and the current section announced on guide pages
- **For everyone:** comfortable line lengths, a sidebar you can hide, light and dark modes, and clean printed pages
- **For privacy:** content on a site you control, with no third-party tracking built in

Embeds such as YouTube videos bring their own tracking, and Keep My Place stays in each reader's own browser.

Accessibility also depends on the content itself – headings, alt text and link text are up to the author (see [Improving the Accessibility of Your Markdown](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2021/09/improving-accessibility-of-markdown/?target=_blank)).
