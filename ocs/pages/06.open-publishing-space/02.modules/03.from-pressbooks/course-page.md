Already have a book in Pressbooks? Start from it, with a free converter (in beta) – no Grav installation needed to see what carries over:

- **[Pressbooks Converter](https://grav-pressbooks-converter.hibbittsdesign.org/)** – upload a Pressbooks XHTML export, and download an Open Publishing Space guide, ready to review and publish

*The same chapter in Pressbooks and in Open Publishing Space, with no revisions – from [Universal Design for Learning (UDL) for Inclusion, Diversity, Equity, and Accessibility (IDEA)](https://ecampusontario.pressbooks.pub/universaldesign2/), CC BY 4.0*

*A module's intro page on a phone: its text and images, then its Learning Outcomes as a Learning Objectives box*

Table: What carries over from a Pressbooks book

| Content | In the converted guide |
|---|---|
| Text, headings, figures and captions | Carried over |
| Exercises and other callouts | Carried over as callout boxes |
| Footnotes | Carried over |
| H5P activities and videos | H5P activities embedded (an option) or linked; other media linked back to the book |
| Tables inserted with a Pressbooks shortcode | Shown as text, to replace with a Markdown table |

Examples (raw conversions, with no revisions or fine-tuning): [four openly licensed Pressbooks books in Open Publishing Space](https://demo.hibbittsdesign.org/variants/grav-open-publishing-quark2-pressbooks-books/)

**No web server needed:** choose Docsify-This or a Starter Kit as the converter's destination, and the book becomes plain Markdown you can adapt, reuse and publish as a website for free – readable right in its GitHub or Codeberg repository, and ready to move to Grav whenever you want – [see the same four books on Docsify-This](https://docsify-this.net/?basePath=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/paulhibbitts/docsify-open-publishing-pressbooks-sites/main&homepage=home.md&sidebar=true&loadSidebar=_sidebar.md&loadNavbar=_navbar.md&pagination=true&searchbox=true&collapsibleSidebarGroups=true&collapseSidebarGroups=true&dark-mode=auto&name=Books%20from%20Pressbooks&edit-link=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/paulhibbitts/docsify-open-publishing-pressbooks-sites/main/home.md&edit-link-text=This%20page%20as%20Markdown&edit-link-emoji=:page_facing_up:) · [on GitHub](https://github.com/paulhibbitts/docsify-open-publishing-pressbooks-sites)
